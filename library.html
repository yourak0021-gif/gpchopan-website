Central Library

Knowledge repository equipped with textbooks, reference volumes, technical journals, and quiet reading areas.

Library Resources & Highlights

  • Comprehensive collection of engineering textbooks across all disciplines.
  • Book Bank facility for SC/ST and economically weaker section students.
  • Subscriptions to national technical magazines, journals, and daily newspapers.
  • Spacious, well-lit reading halls accommodating multiple batches simultaneously.
  • Digital catalog and reference assistance by dedicated library staff.

Library Timings & Regulations

Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Book Issue / Return: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Students are issued library cards at the start of each academic semester. Complete silence and discipline are maintained at all times.
