Central Library

Knowledge repository and reading resources supporting all diploma engineering programs.

About the Central Library

The college library serves as a rich source of knowledge and plays an important role in supporting the academic needs of diploma students. It is well-stocked with textbooks, reference books related to various branches of engineering and applied sciences.

The library provides a quiet and disciplined environment, encouraging students to develop the habit of self-study and broaden their understanding beyond the classroom. With its organized collection and comfortable seating, the library helps students enhance their learning experience and achieve academic excellence.

Timings: Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Closed on Sundays & Govt. Holidays)

Library Highlights & Collections

  • Comprehensive collection of engineering textbooks across all departments.
  • Book Bank facility for SC/ST and economically weaker section students.
  • Subscriptions to national technical journals, periodicals, and daily newspapers.
  • Spacious reading hall with comfortable seating and quiet study environment.
  • Reference books, competitive examination guides, and digital learning materials.
