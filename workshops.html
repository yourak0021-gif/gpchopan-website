Central Workshops & Manufacturing Facilities

Hands-on craftsmanship, machining, fitting, welding, carpentry, and sheet metal fabrication.

About Central Workshops

The Workshop at Government Polytechnic Chopan provides hands-on training in various trades such as fitting, welding, machining, carpentry, and sheet metal work. Equipped with modern tools and machinery, it helps students develop essential practical skills, safety awareness, and craftsmanship. The workshop bridges the gap between theoretical learning and real-world engineering applications, fostering technical competence and innovation among students.

The college workshop is a vital facility where students gain hands-on experience with basic engineering tools, machines, and processes. It provides a safe and organized environment to practice practical skills and apply theoretical knowledge learned in the classroom.

The workshop is divided into different sections equipped with tools and machines for exercises such as cutting, shaping, joining, and assembling materials. These activities help students develop technical skills, precision, and problem-solving abilities essential for any engineering discipline. Students also get practical exposure to general engineering practices, including measurements, material testing, and simple mechanical and electrical exercises.

Workshop Trades & Sections

Specialized shops with industrial grade machine tools

Machine & Turning Shop

Equipped with center lathes, shaping machines, drilling apparatus, and precision measuring tools for metal turning and shaping exercises.

Fitting & Carpentry Shop

Furnished with heavy-duty bench vices, hand files, wood turning lathes, saws, and chisels for precision joint making.

Welding & Sheet Metal Shop

Electric arc welding transformers, oxy-acetylene gas welding kits, sheet cutters, stakes, and protective safety gear.
