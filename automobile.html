Department of Automobile Engineering

Practical engineering in automotive mechanics, IC engines, vehicle chassis, transmission, and modern mobility.

About the Department

The Automobile Engineering branch at Government Polytechnic Chopan is designed to provide comprehensive technical knowledge and practical training in the design, manufacturing, testing, and maintenance of automotive vehicles and systems. The program prepares students to meet the evolving demands of the automotive and mobility sector.

Curriculum Highlights

The curriculum blends theoretical concepts with hands-on applications, covering key areas such as:

Internal Combustion (IC) Engines
Automobile Transmission and Chassis Design
Auto Electrical and Electronic Systems
Thermal Engineering and Heat Transfer
Vehicle Dynamics and Safety Systems
Hydraulics and Pneumatics in Vehicles
Emerging Domains: Electric & Hybrid Vehicles (EV), Sustainable Mobility

Students gain practical exposure in modern laboratories and workshops, where they learn to assemble, disassemble, test, and troubleshoot engines, braking mechanisms, steering systems, and electrical circuits. Industrial visits, workshops, and internships help students connect classroom learning with real-world industry practices.

Career Opportunities: Students of the Automobile Engineering program can pursue careers in automobile manufacturing industries, service and maintenance sectors, research and development, transport corporations, and allied industries, or opt for higher studies to expand their expertise.

Department Laboratories & Workshops

Real automotive test rigs, thermal systems, and mechanics testing units

Thermal Engineering & Hydraulics Laboratory

The Thermal Engineering Laboratory and Hydraulics Laboratory provide students with essential practical knowledge in energy systems and fluid mechanics. In the Thermal Lab, students conduct experiments on heat exchangers, boilers, compressors, and IC engine test rigs to understand thermodynamics, heat transfer, and energy conversion processes. The Hydraulics Lab, equipped with pumps, turbines, and hydraulic trainers, allows students to study fluid properties, pressure, flow, and velocity, along with the practical applications of hydraulic power in machines and automobiles.

Mechanics & Material Testing Laboratory

The Mechanics Laboratory strengthens the foundation of engineering mechanics by offering experiments on forces, moments, friction, simple machines, and material properties like tensile strength and hardness. Through these hands-on activities, students develop a clear understanding of structural stability, motion, and strength of components, which is crucial for designing, analyzing, and maintaining automobile parts and systems.
