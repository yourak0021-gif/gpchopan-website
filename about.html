About Government Polytechnic Chopan

Established in 2016 in Sonbhadra, UP, dedicated to premier government technical education.

Establishment & Affiliation

Established in 2016, Government Polytechnic Chopan, Sonbhadra, is a premier government institution dedicated to technical education. The institute functions under the affiliation of the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow (BTEUP) and is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.

Academic operations initially commenced at the campus of Government Polytechnic Sonbhadra, Lodhi, Robertsganj, before developing its own independent identity and modern campus in Chopan.

Diploma Engineering Programs

The institute offers three core diploma programs:

  • Computer Science and Engineering
  • Electronics Engineering
  • Automobile Engineering

with a strong commitment to providing quality technical education aligned with industry requirements and national development goals.

Holistic Development & Co-Curriculars

Beyond academics, Government Polytechnic Chopan actively fosters co-curricular engagement through sports, cultural events, and social service activities.

This holistic approach helps nurture innovation, discipline, and social responsibility, ensuring students are prepared for meaningful careers as well as lifelong learning. With its focus on excellence, inclusivity, and practical learning, Government Polytechnic Chopan has established itself as one of the most respected polytechnic institutions in Uttar Pradesh.

Institutional Committees & Cells

Click on any committee below to view its objectives, guidelines, and welfare measures

⚖️ SC/ST Committee

The SC/ST Committee at Government Polytechnic Chopan ensures the effective implementation of policies and welfare programs for students and staff belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

Key Objectives:

  • To prevent discrimination and maintain an inclusive, supportive academic environment.
  • To facilitate scholarship assistance and government financial welfare schemes.
  • To address grievances and provide guidance on academic and administrative matters.
  • Book Bank support and specialized learning resources through the central library.

For assistance, students can reach out to the committee coordinator at the administrative office.

🧑‍🏫 Student Counsellor Cell

The Student Counsellor Cell provides personalized mentoring, psychological support, and academic guidance to help students navigate their diploma education and career aspirations.

Services Offered:

  • Academic Counselling: Guidance on subject difficulties, study routines, and exam preparation.
  • Career Mentorship: Advice on diploma specialization, B.Tech lateral entry, and industry jobs.
  • Stress Management: Confidential support for personal, emotional, or transitional challenges.
  • Peer Mentoring: Fostering healthy collaboration between senior and junior batches.

🤝 Institution Industry Cell (IIC)

The Institution Industry Cell bridges the gap between classroom academics and industrial practice by creating active partnerships with industrial manufacturing and IT enterprises.

Activities & Initiatives:

  • Organizing industrial visits to automotive plants, IT hubs, and electronics manufacturing facilities.
  • Facilitating mandatory summer training and internship programs for 2nd and 3rd year students.
  • Inviting corporate guest speakers and technical experts for modern technology workshops.
  • Fostering MoUs for student apprenticeships under NATS / Apprenticeship India.

💼 Training And Placement Cell

The Training and Placement Cell (T&P) is dedicated to facilitating employment opportunities and career readiness for graduating diploma students across all engineering branches.

Placement Assistance:

  • Conducting on-campus and pooled recruitment drives with leading companies.
  • Resume building, mock interviews, and group discussion preparation sessions.
  • Technical test prep and soft skills grooming in the communication lab.
  • Apprenticeship registrations and industry onboarding support.

🌟 Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) continuously monitors, evaluates, and improves academic and administrative benchmarks across Government Polytechnic Chopan.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Curriculum delivery audit and continuous review of laboratory experiments.
  • Collecting student feedback and faculty appraisal for teaching excellence.
  • Encouraging innovative pedagogical tools, digital boards, and smart classrooms.
  • Compliance with AICTE, BTEUP, and state technical education quality standards.

🛡️ Internal Complaint Committee (ICC)

The Internal Complaint Committee (ICC) functions under statutory guidelines (POSH Act) to ensure a safe, secure, and respectful working and learning environment for all female students and staff members.

Key Functions:

  • Zero-tolerance policy towards harassment of any kind.
  • Confidential, impartial, and prompt grievance resolution mechanism.
  • Conducting gender sensitization workshops and awareness programs.
  • Direct online grievance submission via the official feedback portal.

🚫 Anti-Ragging Committee

Government Polytechnic Chopan strictly enforces a Zero Tolerance Policy against ragging in accordance with Supreme Court and AICTE regulations.

Anti-Ragging Regulations:

  • Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, or mental) is strictly prohibited on campus and hostels.
  • Mandatory submission of Anti-Ragging undertakings/affidavits at the time of admission.
  • 24x7 Anti-Ragging flying squads conducting surprise visits in hostel premises.
  • National Anti-Ragging Helpline (Toll-Free): 1800-180-5522.
